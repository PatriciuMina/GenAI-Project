## Notes per question
- **Q1 (most recent event attended; how they heard about it):** [A youth forum] about rethinking education in Romania. Heard about it from members of their organization and on the Instagram page. Then looked up more information on the date and hour, checked their calendar, and enrolled.
- **Q2 (last time looking for a UBB event; where, how they narrowed it down):** Looked on the official websites of the university and the faculties; narrowed events down by their titles, anticipating whether they would be interesting. An event looked interesting because of its subject, the people who were invited, and their own availability.
- **Q3 (what they needed to know about the location; how they found out):** The location and details were clear. Found them on the faculty's website, then on the event's website, and through confirmation and reminder emails and texts.
- **Q4 (last registration form; information and email requirements):** The form asked for name, faculty, year of study, specialization, email address, and where they got the information about the event. There were no requirements on the email address or an account.
- **Q5 (wanted to attend but could not register):** A conference; the registration deadline had passed. Learned that they need to manage their time better and set up reminders. Did not go to the conference.
- **Q6 (last changed or cancelled registration; what happened afterward):** Cancelled through a direct text message to the organisers; the form did not have an option to cancel a registration. The organisers acknowledged the message and said that if they can attend after all, they still can.
- **Q7 (keeping track of details and updates after registering):** Reminders came by email and through a WhatsApp group chat. Details were also posted on the website and on social media pages.
- **Q8 (an event that changed or was cancelled; how they found out):** Found out about the last cancelled event a few hours before it was supposed to start, through text and email.
- **Q9 (most recent arrival at an event; check-in):** There was no check-in and no control of whether participants had filled in the registration form.
- **Q10 (how they joined their organization; roles over time):** Joined through an application, followed by an interview and an assessment centre. Became a team member in [a sales team of one of the organization's departments].
- **Q11 (language of the last university app; effect):** Moodle. It was in English because of their phone's language and the language they study in; the app has an option to change the display language. Recently switched another app's language because it was in German.
- **Q12 (event they decided not to attend; information that affected it):** Did not attend because of their schedule and lack of time. Also, the invited people did not seem interesting enough to make time for the event.
