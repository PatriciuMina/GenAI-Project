⏺ I've read the repo: the code, the TODOs, data-model, the course requirements,
  the midterm drafts 01–08, README, CONTRIBUTING and the templates. Below are
  the open decisions with my recommendation for each. They're also in the plan
  file.

  Already done, so no tickets for these: auth (register, login, JWT), GET 
  /users/me, GET /organizations[/{id}], OrgPermissionService, the whole schema
  with entities and repositories, dev seed, frontend auth, layout and
  organizations pages, Docker, CI, the ADRs, the ER diagram, the midterm drafts
  and the interview guide.

  #: D1
  Decision: W1 MVP
  Recommendation: Includes: the memberships endpoint GET /users/me/memberships
    (from the frontend TODO), a members list, the president adds a member by
    email, changes a role or removes a member, a member can leave, the president

    edits org details, and an admin creates an org and appoints its president.
    Excludes: self-join and join requests.
  ────────────────────────────────────────
  #: D2
  Decision: W2 MVP
  Recommendation: Organizers create a draft, edit it, publish it, then move it
  on
    by hand (close registration → start → complete), with date checks. They can
    also cancel. Excludes: an automatic scheduler and deleting events.
  ────────────────────────────────────────
  #: D3
  Decision: W3 MVP
  Recommendation: A paged GET /events with filters for category, organization,
    date range and title text, sorted by start date. Students see PUBLISHED,
    REGISTRATION_CLOSED and ONGOING events. Only organizers see DRAFT events,
    through GET  /organizations/{id}/events. The details page shows free places
    and the deadline.
  ────────────────────────────────────────
  #: D4
  Decision: W4 MVP
  Recommendation: Register, cancel your own registration, a "My registrations"
    page, and a "registered" badge on event details. No waitlist.
  ────────────────────────────────────────
  #: D5
  Decision: W5 MVP
  Recommendation: Tasks: create, edit/assign, change status (no delete). Also a
    participant list, check-in (no undo) and announcements, which organizers
  post
     and participants/members read. The "Organizing" nav item shows only for
    organizers and presidents.
  ────────────────────────────────────────
  #: D6
  Decision: Granularity
  Recommendation: Split backend and frontend at 3+ points, and keep backend
    tickets at 3 points or less per endpoint group. Feature tickets include
  their
     own basic tests. The type:test tickets add dedicated suites: capacity
    concurrency, the status-transition matrix and the permission matrix.
  ────────────────────────────────────────
  #: D7
  Decision: Diagrams vs. docs overlap
  Recommendation: Section 4.3 of 04 already has a use-case diagram ticket, so it

    becomes the type:diagram ticket rather than a duplicate. There's one
    sequence-diagram ticket per workflow, embedded into the 05 sections, and the

    05 docs tickets depend on them. The state machine and ER reviews happen
  after
     the W2 backend is done.
  ────────────────────────────────────────
  #: D8
  Decision: Docs/report mapping
  Recommendation: One ticket per "Ticket:" marker in 01–05, with the titles and
    points from the files. Section 4.6 is split into W2 (2 pts) and W3 (1 pt),
    which the file itself allows. Sections 6.1, 6.2, 6.4, 6.5 and 6.6 become
    type:research. Section 07 becomes 4 report tickets (7.1–7.4). Plus a final
    review ticket and an "assemble and export to PDF" ticket.
  ────────────────────────────────────────
  #: D9
  Decision: Interviews and personas
  Recommendation: Each interview ticket is 1 student + 1 organizer, worth 2
    points, giving 10 interviews in total. Personas go in a new section 6.7 of
    06-ux-research.md.
  ────────────────────────────────────────
  #: D10
  Decision: Design
  Recommendation: Logo (2 pts), palette + theme.ts (2 pts), 3 wireframe tickets
    and 3 polish tickets (empty states, loading, errors). Wireframes won't block

    the frontend tickets.
  ────────────────────────────────────────
  #: D11
  Decision: Dependencies
  Recommendation: Docs tickets don't wait for features; the final review
    re-checks statuses. The docs chain is: FR → permission matrix → use-case
  list
     → use cases → workflows. The research chain is: interviews → findings →
    assumptions, personas and product flows.
  ────────────────────────────────────────
  #: D12
  Decision: no-genai and QA
  Recommendation: 4 small, self-contained feature tickets get no-genai. QA gets
    one manual-test ticket per workflow. That comes to about 95 tickets and 190
    points, roughly 38 per person.
